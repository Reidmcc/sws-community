# Match rules

Match rules are the modes' own tuning. In the app they're under **Match rules** on Profile and values.

**Fleet Battles:**
- **Morale per kill:** for players, creep, corvettes and frigates, each on offense and on defense. As shipped, creep kills give 1 on offense and 3 on defense.
- **The morale bar:** its kickoff and phase sizes, and its optional drain over time.
- **Timers:** the match and kickoff timers, and respawn time.
- **Creep:** the passive-to-aggressive creep ratio.

**Dogfight:** score to win, match time and pickup timings.

Keys look like `incomschematicattributesconfig_fleetbattle.towpointsgainedforcreepkillwhileondefense`. "tow" is the tug-of-war morale bar.

Each value has a hover tip in the app saying what it does.
